We run into a fun game design quirk here because it's a text-based game. In real life, everyone sees the cards instantly, realizes there is no match, and immediately looks for the high card. In a terminal, the computer calculates the result in milliseconds. To fix this, we need to remove the misleading prompt but keep the 3.5-second `TRIGGER_WINDOW`. This gives players time to read the text, mentally check against the bullseye, and naturally realize "Okay, no match here," before the server steps in and automatically awards the pot to the highest card.
